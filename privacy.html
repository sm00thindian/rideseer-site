Privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026. This policy covers the RideSeer app for Android and this website. "We" means RideSeer. Questions: hello@rideseer.com.

The short version

  • RideSeer has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. We don't sell or share your data for advertising.
  • Your voice is sent only while you're talking to Seer, to understand and answer you.
  • Your location is used to answer questions about where you are and what's ahead.
  • Saved spots, rides, your home and settings stay on your device.
  • Our server keeps no copies of your audio, your words or your location. The one exception: an answer you choose to report is emailed to us.

Each of those services handles the data under its own privacy policy. When you send a place to Google Maps, RideSeer opens Google Maps with that place; what happens next is up to Google Maps.

Microphone

RideSeer listens only while you're talking to Seer: while you hold the talk button, or during a conversation you started with a tap. During a ride, Android may show the microphone as in use because RideSeer keeps it ready for your next press, but audio is sent only while you're talking.

Location

RideSeer uses your location while the app is open, and during a ride also while it runs behind your navigation app, so Seer can answer for where you are and warn you about weather ahead. When the app is closed and no ride is on, it doesn't use your location.

Notification access and "Display over other apps"

If you turn on notification access, RideSeer uses it only to tell whether your navigation app is guiding you and to pause or resume your music. It does not read, store or send the content of your notifications. "Display over other apps" is used only for the floating talk button and to open your navigation app.

Our server

RideSeer's server (hosted on Vercel) passes requests to xAI and Google. For each request it logs which tester's access code was used (by a short name, never the code itself), which feature, and when. It also keeps weekly totals per tester name: rides, minutes of voice, answers and lookups (counts only: the app sends its voice counts every few minutes during a ride and at the end, and the server counts the lookups it makes), for about a year, so we know what RideSeer costs to run. Our hosting provider may also keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for a limited time. We keep no audio, transcripts or locations, except the text of an answer you choose to report (below).

Reporting an answer

If Seer says something offensive, wrong or unsafe, tap the flag in the bottom bar (or Settings → Report Seer’s last answer) and pick a reason. That sends the question, the answer, the reason and the app version to us by email. It includes no location or voice recording. We keep report emails only as long as we need them to fix the problem.

Diagnostics

RideSeer keeps a short log of button presses, connection and audio events on your device to help fix problems. It contains no location, access code or anything you said. It leaves your device only if you choose Settings → Share diagnostics and send it.

Your choices

  • You can turn off microphone, location, notification access and "Display over other apps" in Android settings at any time. Seer needs the microphone and location to work.
  • Delete saved spots and rides in the app's Ride log. Uninstalling RideSeer removes everything it stored on your device.
  • Because we keep no personal content on our server, there is no account or server data to delete. Questions or requests: hello@rideseer.com.

Children

RideSeer is meant for drivers and riders and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date above.
